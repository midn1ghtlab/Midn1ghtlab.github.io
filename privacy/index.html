Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Old Layout for YouTube™ respects your privacy to the highest degree.

  • No Data Collection: This extension does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, user activity, or analytics.
  • Local Storage: The storage permission is used exclusively on your local device to remember if the extension is toggled ON or OFF.
  • No Remote Code: All scripts are contained entirely within the extension package and run locally.
